Your camera, your call.

HalfBeat estimates pulse, breathing and talking from your webcam, then shows how fast your body settles after a hard interview question.

  • Camera only — no microphone, no audio. “Talking” is detected from your face on video, not from sound.
  • No video is stored and nothing leaves this machine. Frames are processed in memory and thrown away; the browser UI is served from 127.0.0.1; the SDK's usage telemetry is switched off in this build.
  • What is saved: if you ask for it, a small numbers-only file on this computer — no images, no audio.
  • Wellness only. These are general-wellness estimates, not a medical measurement or a diagnosis.
  • Stop any time: close this tab, or Ctrl+C in the terminal, and the camera light goes out.
